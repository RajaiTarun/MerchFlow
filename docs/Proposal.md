## Centralized College Merchandise Management System

## 1 Description of the Use Case

In our college, multiple clubs frequently launch their own merchandise such as T-shirts, hoodies, and other items. Currently, each club manages its merchandise independently, typically through separate forms and size charts. As a result, students face several issues, including repeatedly filling out similar forms, experiencing confusion about varying size formats, and dealing with the absence of a single platform to view all available merchandise.

The proposed system, the Centralized College Merchandise Management System, aims to address this problem by acting as a unified portal where all clubs can list and sell their merchandise. Students will be able to create a profile and save their preferred sizes once, eliminating the need to repeatedly enter sizing details for different club merchandise forms.

The primary stakeholders include students, who want a streamlined purchasing experience, and club managers, who need a structured and efficient way to showcase merchandise and manage student orders.

## 2 Key Functionalities

## Unified Merchandise Catalog

Students can view merchandise from multiple clubs in one place, allowing them to easily browse, compare, and purchase

available items.

## Saved Size Profile

Students can create a profile and save their preferred size, enabling faster ordering and minimizing confusion about size formats across different clubs.

## Delivery & Distribution Updates

Students will receive real-time updates and notifications regarding upcoming delivery and distribution time slots for their orders, ensuring they never miss a pickup.

## Club Dashboard for Merchandise Management

A dedicated interface for clubs to publish and showcase merchandise, manage student orders efficiently, and reach a wider student audience.

## User Interaction

Users will interact with the system through a web-based interface accessible via browsers. Students can browse merchandise, place orders, and receive delivery updates, while club administrators manage merchandise listings and orders through a dashboard.

## 4 Domain

The project belongs to the E-Commerce / Education Technology domain. It focuses on improving the process of selling and managing college merchandise by providing a centralized platform for multiple clubs. The system simplifies merchandise browsing, ordering, and delivery updates for students while helping clubs manage orders and distribution more efficiently within the campus ecosystem.
